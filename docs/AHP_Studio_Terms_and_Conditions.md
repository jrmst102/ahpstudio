## 2. Description of the Software

AHP Studio is an open-source, web-based decision support application that implements the Analytic Hierarchy Process (AHP). The Software enables users to define multi-criteria decision problems, perform pairwise comparisons, compute priority vectors and consistency ratios, synthesize global rankings, and conduct sensitivity analyses. AHP Studio is developed and maintained by Dr. Jose Mendoza for use in graduate-level academic instruction.

## 3. Intellectual Foundation and Academic Attribution

### 3.1 The Analytic Hierarchy Process

AHP Studio is based on the Analytic Hierarchy Process (AHP), a multi-criteria decision-making methodology invented by **Dr. Thomas L. Saaty** (1926–2017). Dr. Saaty was a Distinguished University Professor at the University of Pittsburgh's Joseph M. Katz Graduate School of Business and a member of the National Academy of Engineering. He first described the mathematical foundations of AHP in a 1977 paper published in the *Journal of Mathematical Psychology* and subsequently in his seminal 1980 book, *The Analytic Hierarchy Process: Planning, Priority Setting, Resource Allocation* (McGraw-Hill).

The AHP provides a structured framework for organizing complex decisions into hierarchies, quantifying the relative importance of criteria through pairwise comparisons on a fundamental 1–9 ratio scale, deriving priority weights via the principal eigenvector of comparison matrices, measuring judgment consistency through the Consistency Index and Consistency Ratio, and synthesizing local priorities into global rankings of alternatives. Dr. Saaty's work has been applied across fields including strategic planning, resource allocation, conflict resolution, environmental management, healthcare, and defense. His contributions have been cited over 200,000 times in the academic literature.

### 3.2 Required Attribution

All users, contributors, and distributors of AHP Studio must acknowledge the intellectual foundation of the AHP methodology. Any published work, academic paper, thesis, presentation, or report that presents results obtained through AHP Studio must include a citation to Dr. Saaty's foundational work. The following citation is recommended:

> Saaty, T. L. (1980). *The Analytic Hierarchy Process: Planning, Priority Setting, Resource Allocation.* New York: McGraw-Hill.

Additional relevant citations include:

> Saaty, T. L. (1977). A scaling method for priorities in hierarchical structures. *Journal of Mathematical Psychology*, 15(3), 234–281.

> Saaty, T. L. (2008). Decision making with the analytic hierarchy process. *International Journal of Services Sciences*, 1(1), 83–98.

Users are further encouraged to acknowledge that AHP Studio draws conceptual inspiration from Expert Choice, the commercial AHP software created by Thomas L. Saaty and Ernest Forman in 1983.
